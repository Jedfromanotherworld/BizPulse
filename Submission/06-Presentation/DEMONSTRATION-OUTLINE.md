# BizPulse presentation and live demonstration

1. Introduction: BizPulse, a Small Business Management Application for owners and cashiers. Introduce actual team members and responsibilities.
2. Problem: fragmented stock and sales records can cause missed deductions and unreliable summaries.
3. Target users: small retailers and their cashiers using a single Android device.
4. Major features: product/category records, POS, stock deduction, receipts, analytics and audit history.
5. UI/UX: explain emerald/slate colors, consistent controls, role-based navigation, dark mode and touch targets. Show real screenshots.
6. Live demonstration: log in as owner; show dashboard; add a product with an existing barcode; search or scan it in POS; check out with enough cash; show receipt, reduced stock and analytics. Log in as cashier and explain restricted controls. Demonstrate invalid input safely.
7. Technologies: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room/SQLite, Hilt, DataStore, Coroutines and ZXing. Explain the ViewModel/repository separation and atomic checkout.
8. Challenges: accurate stock and receipt history, role checks, malformed imports and small-screen layouts. Do not claim personal work you cannot explain.
9. Testing: show the supplied historical unit-test reports and your completed device test matrix. Explain that compiled instrumentation tests are not proof of device execution.
10. Future improvements: controlled owner enrollment, stronger password hashing, precise monetary storage, secure backups and broader device testing.
